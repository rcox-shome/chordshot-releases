# ChordShot privacy

ChordShot captures only when you start a selection. Screenshots go to your Mac's clipboard and/or the folder you choose. Silent recordings save in that folder's Recordings subfolder. ChordShot does not upload captures, record microphone or system audio, create an account, or include analytics.

Accessibility access lets ChordShot recognize the mouse/trackpad capture gesture and its Space, Escape, and stop controls. It does not store your keystrokes. Screen Recording access allows macOS to provide the selected screen content. Start at login is optional.

The Sparkle updater contacts GitHub to check for updates and download app releases. Automatic checks are your choice; you can change the setting in ChordShot. GitHub receives ordinary connection information, such as an IP address. ChordShot disables Sparkle's optional system profile submission. Captures are never attached to these requests.

Capture files and clipboard content remain subject to the permissions, clipboard managers, backup, and folder-sync services configured on your Mac. Deleting the app does not delete your saved captures.
