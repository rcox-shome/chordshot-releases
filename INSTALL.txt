ChordShot for Mac — macOS 15.2 or newer

1. Drag ChordShot into Applications, then open it there.
2. Use Enable… to grant Accessibility and Screen Recording in System Settings.
3. If macOS requests it, quit and reopen ChordShot from Applications.

Mouse: hold left + right together and drag. Release to take a screenshot.
Trackpad: hold Control + Option, then click and drag with one finger.
Change the trackpad shortcut in Settings if you prefer Option alone.

Video: press Space during selection, then release. After the countdown,
the red REC control shows recording is active. Click Stop or press Command–Shift–X.
Videos are silent. Screenshots copy to the clipboard and save as PNG by default.
Captures save in ~/Pictures/ChordShot; videos use its Recordings subfolder.

ChordShot lives in your menu bar. Use its menu to open Settings or Check for Updates.
Keep one installed copy in Applications. Future updates use the same signed identity.
macOS may still ask for approval after system policy changes or revoked access.

This is an initial beta. Please report issues at:
https://github.com/rcox-shome/chordshot-releases/issues

For users of the earlier development builds: switching to the public release
changes signing identity and may require one final permission approval.
Quit the old app after saving any recording before replacing it.
